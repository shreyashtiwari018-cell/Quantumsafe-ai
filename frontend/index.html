    How QuantumSafe AI works

    See the complete safety-intelligence workflow from an unstructured report to actionable investigation and recurring-pattern insights.

    01 • REPORT INTELLIGENCESubmit a workplace safety report and let the NLP engine extract hazards, activity, location, barriers and key signals.
    02 • RISK ASSESSMENTAssess risk level and SIF potential, then surface why the report was flagged and what action is recommended.
    03 • PATTERN INTELLIGENCECombine reports to identify recurring hazards, locations and failed barriers that may indicate systemic exposure.
    04 • INVESTIGATIONOpen an individual report to review the evidence, recommendation, pattern signal and investigation guidance.

    QUANTUMSAFE AI • RISK ASSESSMENT

    Safety Risk Assessment

    Review the core factors QuantumSafe AI uses to prioritize a safety observation and identify potential Serious Injury & Fatality exposure.

    SIF POTENTIALIdentify conditions that could lead to serious injury or fatality.
    HAZARD SEVERITYAssess the type and potential consequence of the hazard.
    BARRIER FAILURESurface failed or missing controls that allowed the exposure.
    RISK PRIORITYCombine safety signals to highlight cases needing faster review.
    RISK PRIORITY MODEL
    CRITICALSIF potential + severe exposure
    HIGHMajor exposure or significant barrier failure
    MEDIUMMeaningful hazard requiring corrective action
    LOWLower consequence / controlled exposure
